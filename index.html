Official Rules
📍 Drops

All drops will be played laterally — drop your ball on whatever line it went into the hazard, no closer to the hole.

⛳ Gimmes

Any putt within a normal putter length is a gimme. No giraffe putter.

🌿 Gallery Drops

Gallery drops can be taken anywhere that is not out of bounds or a hazard area that is waist height and lower. Gallery drop must be taken in fescue area.

🌾Ball in heather (waist down) — free gallery drop, no penalty.
💧Ball in water — drop and +1 stroke penalty.
🌲Ball lost in woods — drop and +1 stroke penalty.
🔍 Lost Ball

If you hit your ball in the general direction of water, OB, or the woods and cannot find it, you must assume it went in and take a drop with a +1 stroke penalty.

✋ Lift, Clean & Place

Lift, clean and place is in effect in all areas outside of bunkers. If the bunkers are rough, lift, rake, and place.

⚡ R1 Stableford — Max Score

Double bogey is the maximum score on any hole. Wherever you are on the hole when you reach double bogey, pick up to keep pace of play.
